Effective Usage

It is designed to provide information about the jurisdiction of the user country, whether it is legal advice, exploring case precedents, or researching acts and case laws. How does Lawify work?

Lawify utilizes a highly trained LLM, optimized specifically for legal queries through hyper-tuning. Users select their desired region, triggering a tailored chat session based on that region's legal framework. Lawify then loads pertinent legal documents, including constitutions, codes of conduct, laws, and recent amendments relevant to the selected region.

Our platform integrates a comprehensive database of legal resources, such as statutory laws, judicial precedents, regulatory guidelines, and other legal texts. This ensures the model has a vast repository for accurate and relevant responses. Lawify's strength lies in its region-specific training, involving extensive familiarization with local legal terminologies, procedures, and contextual nuances, guaranteeing accurate and contextually appropriate answers.
