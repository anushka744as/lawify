Bringing Convenience

Using Lawify is as simple as typing your query into the input box in any worldwide dialect (the chatbot is proficient in most international languages) and hitting enter. The chatbot will then output an answer within seconds, depending on how detailed the question is. Lawify takes variables such as state, religion, age, and gender and can answer follow-up questions.

Effective Usage

It is designed to provide information about the jurisdiction of the user country, whether it is legal advice, exploring case precedents, or researching acts and case laws. The more precise and intricate a prompt, the better Lawify can tailor its response to the users’ needs. Asking general questions will yield general advice, which may be counterproductive. Lawify will only outline available options rather than guide users or ask them to choose one. It is not meant to be used inside a courtroom.

How does Lawify work?

Lawify utilizes OpenAI's ChatGPT 4.0 API, optimized specifically for legal queries through hyper-tuning. Users select their desired region, triggering a tailored chat session based on that region's legal framework. Lawify then loads pertinent legal documents, including constitutions, codes of conduct, laws, and recent amendments relevant to the selected region.

Our platform integrates a comprehensive database of legal resources, such as statutory laws, judicial precedents, regulatory guidelines, and other legal texts. This ensures the model has a vast repository for accurate and relevant responses. Lawify's strength lies in its region-specific training, involving extensive familiarization with local legal terminologies, procedures, and contextual nuances, guaranteeing accurate and contextually appropriate answers.
